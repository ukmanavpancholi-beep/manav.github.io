A local barber, easy to reach

Stanmore Barbers is a local grooming shop offering men's haircuts and styling. Stanmore has a strong barbering community, and our focus is the same things people value most: friendly service, careful technique and a clean, modern finish.

Whether you want a classic cut or a sharp fade, you can expect a professional atmosphere built around quality craftsmanship and customer satisfaction.

Getting here

We are on Stanmore Hill, well connected by public transport. Stanmore Station has direct Jubilee line links to London, so it is easy to fit a haircut around your day.
